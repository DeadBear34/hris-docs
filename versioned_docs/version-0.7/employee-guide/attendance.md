# Attendance

Go to **Modul Absensi › Absensi Saya** (My Attendance).

## Check in and check out

1. When you arrive, select **Absen Masuk** (Check In).
2. When you leave, select **Absen Pulang** (Check Out). You can only check out after checking in on the same day.

All times follow office time (WIB).

## On time, late, and absent

Your work schedule sets your start time, a short grace period, and the time check-in closes. With the default schedule (08:00 start, 5-minute grace period, check-in closes at 08:10):

| You check in | Recorded as |
| --- | --- |
| 08:00–08:05 | Present |
| 08:06–08:10 | Late, with the minutes counted from 08:00 |
| After 08:10 | Check-in is closed and the day is recorded as absent |

Your department may use a different schedule. Your own schedule is shown on the attendance page.

## When you can't check in

Check-in is not available when:

- check-in has already closed for the day;
- the day is not a working day in your schedule;
- it is a public holiday or collective leave;
- you are on approved leave;
- you have already checked in today.

## Offline check-in

If your connection drops, you can still check in. HRIS saves the time you pressed the button and sends it as soon as you are back online. Offline check-ins must be sent on the same day and are marked as offline in your history. HR reviews offline check-ins regularly.

## Days you didn't check in

At the end of each working day, days without a check-in are recorded as absent, unless it was a holiday or you were on approved leave. If a record is wrong, contact HR; HR can correct it, and the correction and its reason are recorded.
