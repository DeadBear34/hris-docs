# Notifications

Select the bell icon in the top bar to open your notifications. The number on the bell shows how many you haven't read. New notifications appear instantly while HRIS is open.

## What you are notified about

| You receive a notification when… | Who receives it |
| --- | --- |
| Your leave request is approved or rejected | You |
| A leave request needs your decision | Managers and HR who can approve it |
| A leave request is assigned to you after an organization change | The new approving manager |
| A leave request you were notified about is decided | Managers and HR involved |
| A new account is waiting for approval | HR |

## Completed tasks

When a task is done, for example someone else already decided a leave request, its notification is marked as read automatically. It stays in your history but no longer counts toward the badge.

Select a notification to open the related page, or use **Tandai Semua Dibaca** (Mark all as read) on the notifications page to clear the badge.
