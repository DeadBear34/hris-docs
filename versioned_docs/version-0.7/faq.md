# Frequently Asked Questions

### I didn't receive the verification code.

Check your spam folder, then request a new code from the verification page. You can request a new code once a minute; only the most recent code works.

### Why can't I request leave on a certain date?

The date may already be full for your department, it may overlap one of your existing requests, or the leave type may require more notice. The form explains which rule applies. See [Requesting leave](employee-guide/requesting-leave.md).

### My manager is wrong. How do I fix it?

Your manager follows from your position in the organization structure. Ask HR to check your position or the structure. See [Organization structure](hr-guide/organization-structure.md).

### Why was my check-in rejected?

Check-in may have closed for the day, the day may not be a working day in your schedule, or you may be on leave or a holiday. See [Attendance](employee-guide/attendance.md).

### I see "Versi baru tersedia" (New version available). What should I do?

Finish what you are doing, then select **Muat ulang** (Reload). HRIS never reloads on its own, so your input is safe until you choose to update.

### A page asks me to reload because the data changed.

Someone else updated the same record while you were editing it. Reload to see the latest version, then make your change again. This prevents changes from being overwritten.

### I see a menu missing that a colleague has.

Menus depend on your position. See [Roles and access](getting-started/roles-and-access.md).

### How do I report a problem?

Contact HR or the HRIS team with what you were doing, the time, and a screenshot. Never share your password or a screenshot that shows your session details.
