# Signing In

## Getting an account

You get an HRIS account in one of two ways:

- **HR creates it for you.** You receive your email address and an initial password. You will be asked to choose a new password the first time you sign in.
- **You register yourself.** Open [hris.click](https://hris.click), select **Register**, and follow the steps below.

### Registering yourself

1. Fill in your full name, work email, phone number (starting with the country code, for example `+62`), gender, and a password.
2. Accept the terms and submit.
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email. The code is valid for 10 minutes; you can request a new one if it expires.
4. Wait for HR to approve your account. You can sign in once it is approved.

Your password must be 8 to 72 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol.

## Signing in

Enter your email and password on the sign-in page. You stay signed in for up to 24 hours.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Incorrect email or password | Check both and try again. Use **Lupa Password** (Forgot password) if needed |
| Your email is not verified yet | Enter the verification code sent to your email |
| Your account is still waiting for approval | Wait for HR to approve your account |
| Your account has been deactivated | Contact HR |

After several failed attempts, sign-in is paused briefly. Wait a minute before trying again.

## Forgot your password?

Select **Lupa Password?** (Forgot password) on the sign-in page, enter your email, and open the reset link we send you. The link is valid for 15 minutes. After you set a new password, you are signed out on every device.

## Signing out

Signing out ends your session on the current device immediately. To sign out on every device at once, change your password.

## Installing HRIS as an app

HRIS can be installed like an app:

- **Phone:** open hris.click and choose **Add to Home Screen** from the browser menu.
- **Computer:** choose **Install** from the browser's address bar.

When a new version of HRIS is available, a **Versi baru tersedia** (New version available) banner appears. Select **Muat ulang** (Reload) when you are ready; HRIS never reloads by itself, so you won't lose anything you are typing.
