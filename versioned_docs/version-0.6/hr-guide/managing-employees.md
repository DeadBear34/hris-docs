# Managing Employees

Add employees from **Karyawan › Tambah Karyawan** or import them from a spreadsheet with **Karyawan › Impor CSV**.

In 0.6, the department, position, and manager of each employee are set **independently and by hand**:

- departments are a simple list;
- positions are shared across the whole company;
- the manager you choose is the person who approves the employee's leave.

When a manager leaves or changes role, update the manager of each of their employees one by one. From 0.7, managers follow automatically from the organization structure; see the [latest guide](pathname:///docs/hr-guide/organization-structure).

HRIS prevents circular assignments, such as two employees being each other's manager.
