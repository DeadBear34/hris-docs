# Signing In

You get an account in one of two ways:

- **HR creates it for you** and gives you an initial password, which you change the first time you sign in.
- **You register yourself** at [hris.click](https://hris.click): fill in your details, enter the 6-digit code sent to your email, and wait for HR to approve your account.

Sign in with your email and password. You stay signed in for up to 24 hours. If you forget your password, select **Lupa Password?** (Forgot password) on the sign-in page.
