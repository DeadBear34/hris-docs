# Requesting Leave

Go to **Cuti (Leave)** and select **Ajukan Cuti** (Request Leave). Choose the leave type, dates, and an optional reason, then submit. Your balance is reserved immediately.

- Only working days count; weekends and holidays are excluded.
- Past dates are not allowed, except for sick leave.
- You can't have two active requests on the same dates.
- **New in 0.6:** if too many colleagues in your department are already on approved leave on a date, that date can't be requested.

## Who approves my leave?

The manager HR assigned to you approves your leave. If you have no assigned manager, HR decides your requests.

:::note
In 0.6, a pending request stays with the manager it was sent to, even if your manager changes later. HR can still decide it. From 0.7, pending requests move to your new manager automatically.
:::

## Track and cancel

Requests are pending, approved, rejected, or cancelled. Rejected and cancelled requests return the reserved balance. You can cancel a pending request at any time and an approved request until its first day.
