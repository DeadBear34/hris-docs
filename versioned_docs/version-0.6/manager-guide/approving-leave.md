# Approving Leave

Go to **Persetujuan Cuti** (Leave Approvals). You see requests from employees HR has assigned to you as their manager.

Open a request to see its details and the colleagues in the same department who are on leave at the same time, then select **Setujui** (Approve) or **Tolak** (Reject).

- If approving would exceed the department's limit of people on leave at the same time, approval is blocked. HR can approve in exceptional cases with a written reason.
- You can't decide your own leave.

:::note
In 0.6, HR is not always notified of new leave requests, and approval notifications disappear once a request is decided. Both are improved in 0.7.
:::
