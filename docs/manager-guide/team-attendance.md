# Team Attendance

Go to **Modul Absensi › Absensi Tim** (Team Attendance) to see the attendance of your direct reports.

Your direct reports are the employees whose positions report to yours in the organization structure, plus anyone HR has assigned to you directly.

For each employee and day you can see the status (present, late, absent, on leave, or holiday), check-in and check-out times, and whether the check-in was recorded offline. Use the filters to change the period.

If a record looks wrong, ask HR to correct it. Corrections are made by HR and are always recorded with a reason.
