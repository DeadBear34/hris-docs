# Requesting Leave

Go to **Cuti (Leave)** in the sidebar.

## Submit a request

1. Select **Ajukan Cuti** (Request Leave).
2. Choose the leave type, the start and end dates, and an optional reason.
3. Check the calendar: dates where your department has already reached its leave limit are marked as full.
4. Submit. Your balance for that leave type is reserved immediately.

Some leave types need a supporting document, for example a doctor's note for sick leave longer than one day. Upload it as a photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB) from the request details while it is still pending.

### Rules to know

- **Working days only.** Weekends and public holidays are not counted. Leave from Friday to Monday counts as two days.
- **No past dates**, except for sick leave.
- **Notice and length.** Some leave types must be requested a number of days in advance or have a maximum length per request. The form tells you when a rule applies.
- **No overlaps.** You can't have two active requests on the same dates.
- **Department limit.** If too many colleagues in your department are already on approved leave on a date, that date can't be requested.

## Who approves my leave?

Your **line manager** approves your leave. HRIS works out who that is from your position in the organization; you don't need to choose anyone. If your manager is away, HR can decide the request instead.

If the organization changes while your request is pending (for example, your manager moves to another role), your request is passed to your new manager automatically.

## Track and cancel

Your requests and their status are listed on the same page:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting for a decision |
| Approved | Approved; your attendance for those days is marked as leave |
| Rejected | Not approved; the reserved balance is returned |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by you; the reserved balance is returned |

You can cancel a pending request at any time, and an approved request until its first day. Uploaded documents are deleted when you cancel.

## Your balance

Your annual leave allowance is added automatically at the start of each year. The balance view shows how much you have left; days for pending requests are already set aside, so you can't overbook.

If you think your balance is wrong, contact HR. HR can make corrections, and every change is recorded.
