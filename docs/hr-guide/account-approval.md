# Account Approval

People who register themselves can't sign in until HR approves them. You are notified when a new account has verified its email and is waiting for you.

1. Go to **Persetujuan Akun** (Account Approvals).
2. Check that the person is a genuine employee.
3. Select **Setujui** (Approve).

Only accounts with a verified email appear in the list.

To stop someone from signing in later, deactivate their account from their employee page. Their pending leave requests and any approvals assigned to them are moved to the right people automatically.
