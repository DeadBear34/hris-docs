# Roles and Access

What you can do in HRIS depends on your **position**, not on who you are. Each position comes with a set of access rights. When you move to another position, your access changes with it.

Every employee can:

- request leave and see their own balance;
- check in and check out, and see their own attendance;
- update their own profile and photo;
- receive notifications.

## Access by position

| Position | Additional access |
| --- | --- |
| Lead, Supervisor, and Manager | Approve leave of direct reports; see their attendance |
| C-level (CEO, CTO, COO, CPO) | Approve leave of direct reports; view all employees, leave, and attendance |
| HR Manager | Manage employees and the organization, approve any leave, manage leave types, holidays, and balances, correct attendance, approve new accounts, and view the activity log |
| HR Supervisor and HR officers | Add and update employees; view all employees, leave, and attendance |
| Administrator | Everything, including the access settings themselves |

These are the default settings. Administrators can adjust the access of each position on the **Matriks Fitur** (Feature Matrix) page.

## Missing a menu?

If a page or menu you need is not visible, your position does not have that access. Ask HR or an administrator to review your position's access rather than sharing another person's account.
