**Feature release.** Department leave limits.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Department leave limit.** HR can set how many people in a department may be on leave on the same day. Full dates are shown on the leave calendar.
- Leave types for unplanned absence, such as sick leave, can be exempted from the limit.
- Managers see which colleagues are on leave at the same time when reviewing a request.

## Fixed

- The HR leave overview now lists every request.
