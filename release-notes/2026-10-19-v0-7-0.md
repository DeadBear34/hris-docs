**Feature release.** Organization structure and automatic line managers.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Organization structure.** Units can now be nested (company, directorates, departments), and each position belongs to a unit and reports to another position. See [Organization structure](pathname:///docs/hr-guide/organization-structure).
- **Automatic line managers.** Each employee's manager follows from their position. When a manager leaves, whoever takes over the position takes over the team.
- **Organization chart** under **Struktur Organisasi**.
- The employee form previews the line manager as soon as a position is selected.

## Improved

- Pending leave requests move to the new manager automatically when the organization changes, so they no longer get stuck.
- Signing out ends your session on the server immediately.
- Positions can have a maximum number of holders, for example one head per unit.

## Fixed

- Everyone who can approve a leave request, including HR, is now notified about it.
- Approval notifications are kept as history and marked as read when the request is decided, instead of disappearing.

## Good to know

- Every position now belongs to a unit. HR should assign existing positions to their units under **Position**.
